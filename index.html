Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Ski Queens collects and uses personal information when you visit skiqueens.co.uk, send us an enquiry, sign up to our newsletter or book a service with us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the Data Protection Act 2018 and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Who we are

Ski Queens is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at any time through our Contact page.

The information we collect

  • Enquiries and contact messages — your name, email address, phone number if you choose to give it, the subject of your enquiry, your message, and the page or service you were viewing when you contacted us.
  • Newsletter sign-ups — your email address.
  • Bookings and purchases — the details needed to arrange your stay, service, Instagram feature or brand partnership, such as dates, number of guests and billing contact. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card details.
  • Technical information — when you visit the site, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange the stay, service or collaboration you ask about — because you have asked us to (steps before a contract, or the contract itself).
  • To send our newsletter — with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • To keep records for accounting, tax and legal purposes — because the law requires it.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly, and to prevent spam — in our legitimate interests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Who we share it with

We only share your information where it is needed to provide our services:

  • Chalet owners and service providers — such as drivers, nannies, chefs, instructors and helicopter operators — only the details needed to fulfil a booking you have requested.
  • Website hosting and forms — Netlify, which hosts this site and receives form submissions.
  • Payments — Stripe, which processes card payments securely.
  • Email — our email provider, which delivers enquiry notifications and our replies.
  • Professional advisers and authorities — where we are required to by law.

International transfers

Some of our providers are based in, or store data in, the United States and other countries outside the UK and European Economic Area. Where this happens, we rely on recognised safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking — up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Booking and payment records — six years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to ask for it to be transferred, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority in your own country, such as the CNIL in France.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection, and access to enquiries and booking information is limited to the people who need it to help you.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Details about children that you share with us — for example, for a nanny booking — are used only to arrange that service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Cookie policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. This policy explains what we use on skiqueens.co.uk.

Our approach

Ski Queens does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or cookies that follow you around the web. We do not set any cookies for analytics or marketing purposes, which is why you do not see a cookie banner when you visit.

Strictly necessary technology

Our hosting provider may use technology that is essential for delivering the site securely, such as protecting forms against spam. This does not identify you personally and cannot be switched off, because the site would not work without it. The law allows this without asking for consent.

Third-party services

  • Fonts — our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address, as with any web request. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Instagram, YouTube and partner websites — links on our site take you to other websites, which have their own cookie and privacy policies. Those sites only set cookies once you visit them.
  • Payments — if you pay us online, Stripe may use cookies on its checkout pages to process your payment securely and prevent fraud.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Guidance for the most common browsers is available at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we ever introduce optional cookies, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.
