Ski resorts opening dates

Season 2026/2027 — the opening dates to know across the iconic resorts.

By Ski Queens · 7 November 2026

* * *
Austria

Kitzbühel

The first run of the season opens on the Resterkogel on 6 November 2026, with more of the ski area following as the snow builds.

  • Opens 6 November 2026
Switzerland

Gstaad

Glacier 3000 opens first, on 7 November 2026, with skiing until 30 April 2027.

  • Opens 7 November 2026
  • Glacier 3000 until 30 April 2027
France

Val Thorens

The highest resort in the 3 Vallées opens on 21 November 2026, with skiing until 2 May 2027.

  • Opens 21 November 2026
Switzerland

St. Moritz

Corvatsch opens on 21 November 2026, followed by Corviglia.

  • Corvatsch opens 21 November 2026
United States

Aspen

Aspen Mountain and Snowmass open on 26 November 2026.

  • Opens 26 November 2026
Switzerland

Zermatt

The glacier skis all year, and the wider ski area opens for the season in November 2026.

  • Glacier skiing all year
France

Val d'Isère

Val d'Isère opens on 28 November 2026, with skiing until 2 May 2027.

  • Opens 28 November 2026
Japan

Niseko

Niseko Grand Hirafu opens on 28 November 2026, with night skiing from 12 December.

  • Opens 28 November 2026
Italy

Cortina

The Dolomiti Superski season begins on 28 November 2026.

  • Opens 28 November 2026
France

Courchevel

Courchevel opens on 4 December 2026, with the 3 Vallées links opening the following day.

  • Opens 4 December 2026
  • Until 18 April 2027
* * *

Dates can move with the snow, so always check with the resort before you travel. The new season is loading.
