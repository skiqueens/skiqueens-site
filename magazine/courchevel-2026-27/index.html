Just Opened · Courchevel

What's new in Courchevel for 2026–2027

The hotels, tables and new names to know before the season. New addresses — the same Courchevel magic.

By Ski Queens · 7 November 2026

* * *
Hotel · Courchevel 1850

Saint Roch

Reopening on 18 December 2026, transformed by Maisons Pariente. The new Saint Roch sits on the Bellecôte piste, at the foot of the slopes and a short walk from the village.

  • 37 rooms and suites
  • On the Bellecôte piste
  • Opens 18 December 2026
Spa · Saint Roch

Tata Harper Spa

A new wellness retreat at Hôtel Saint Roch, built around an indoor pool and natural Tata Harper treatments.

Hotel · Courchevel 1850

Bourbon Hotel

A new address from Chapitre Six with the spirit of a London private club — velvets, dark woods and deep armchairs by Studio Henry — and direct access to the slopes of Les 3 Vallées. A cocktail and vinyl bar and a contrast-therapy spa complete the picture.

  • 28 rooms and 3 suites
  • Ski-in/ski-out
  • Opens 18 December 2026
Restaurant · Bourbon Hotel

Simonetta

An Italian-inspired mountain trattoria from chef Simone Zanoni, known for his Michelin-starred cooking at Le George in Paris — and his first table in the mountains.

Restaurant · La Sivolière

ZIA

The Italian favourite arrives at La Sivolière, led by chefs Manon Santini and Rocco Seminara, with crudos, pizzette and aged meats from an open kitchen, from breakfast to dinner.

Hotel · Courchevel Moriond

Steamboat

A new five-star address from Alpine Resorts on the main axis of Moriond, with suites and apartments for four to fourteen guests, a heated panoramic pool, sauna and hammam.

  • Suites and apartments
  • Opening for winter 2026/27
Restaurant · Steamboat

ARVI

A new Savoyard table by chef Frédéric Steiger — fondue, raclette and charcuterie — with a bar for après-ski.

Hotel · Courchevel Moriond

Sundance

A cascading hamlet of chalets above the Courchevel valley, with five-star apartments and penthouses, a 16-metre indoor pool and the Italian restaurant La Bokka.

  • 20 apartments and penthouses
  • 16-metre indoor pool
  • Opening for winter 2026/27
Hotel & Spa · Courchevel La Tania

Bârma

A new hotel and spa at the foot of the slopes in Courchevel La Tania, with a spa by Susanne Kaufmann.

  • Opens 5 December 2026
  • Spa by Susanne Kaufmann
